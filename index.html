Yingguang Zhang 张英广

Associate Professor of Finance

I am a financial economist specializing in asset pricing. My research studies expectations, information, behavioral finance, real estate, and the use of artificial intelligence and machine learning in financial markets.

I received my Ph.D. in Finance from the USC Marshall School of Business and my B.A. degrees in Economics and Statistics from UC Berkeley.
